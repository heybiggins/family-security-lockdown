# Family Security Lockdown

A step-by-step skill that has your AI assistant walk you and your family through locking down your digital life, one area at a time. It covers passwords, two-factor sign-in, old accounts, identity, phone, home network, computers, browser, finances (optional), and family. Then it keeps checking in until the work is done.

## Use it

- **Claude (Agent Skills):** put this folder in your skills directory, or upload `SKILL.md` as a skill.
- **ChatGPT, Gemini, or any other AI:** paste the contents of `SKILL.md` into a new chat, a custom GPT, a Gem, or your project instructions. Then say "Start the security lockdown."

The assistant asks a few setup questions and shows a ten-item checklist. It works through each area with current setting paths for the services you use. It never asks you to paste passwords, codes, or recovery keys.
